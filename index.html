OUR PHILOSOPHY

Forged in History. Resurrected through Ancient Fire.

We do not merely manufacture replicas; we resurrect lost civilizations. Our philosophy stands against mass production, automation and computer-driven metallurgy: transcendent art is born the way it was created 2,000 years ago, through the human hand and organic elements.

  • Master tools, chisels and anvils forged from verified ancient iron
  • Casting molds hand-carved by master woodcarvers with 40+ years of experience
  • Led by pupils of the School of Slavay of Haskovo
  • Broken Mold policy: the original mold is destroyed on completion
