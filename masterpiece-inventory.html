THE SACRED LAW OF THE UNIQUE ARTIFACT

The Broken Mold Policy

Our masterpieces are made strictly on individual request. Because of the astronomical value of raw materials and the round-the-clock manual labor involved, we hold no inventory of duplicates. Every piece is a definitive, absolute unique artifact: a strict series of “1-of-1”.

The moment the precious metal cools and the master jeweler finishes the final micro-detail, the original mold is permanently destroyed in front of the artisans. A second piece will never exist.

  • Tools, chisels and matrices forged from authentic ancient iron
  • Molds hand-carved by woodcarvers of the Tryavna, Teteven and Troyan schools
  • Metal composition verified by handheld XRF spectrometer
  • Each work delivered with a Certificate of the Sacred Status of the Unique Artifact
