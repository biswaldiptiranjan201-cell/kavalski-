Geophysics & Aerial Surveys

Airborne geophysical surveys and defense-grade subsurface intelligence for B2B and B2G clients, delivered with millimeter accuracy.

FLAGSHIP DIVISION

Non-Destructive 3D Mapping of What Lies Below

Kavalski Company-USA combines cutting-edge Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS/UAV) with highly sensitive sensors for airborne ground-penetrating radar and magnetometry. The result is non-destructive three-dimensional mapping of subsurface anomalies, underground tunnels, concealed critical infrastructure and historical sites.

Operations are conducted globally to support large-scale infrastructure development, strategic agricultural planning, raw material resource tracking and the documentation of high-significance cultural heritage sites.

CORE TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Four Ways We Look Underground

Airborne GPR

Localization and detection of non-metallic voids, tunnels, underground bunkers and ancient structures at depths exceeding 3–5 meters, without excavation.

Aerial Magnetometry

Precise mapping of buried metallic targets, pipelines, underground utility infrastructure and unexploded ordnance (UXO).

Infrastructure & Engineering

Land scanning prior to construction, slope stability analysis, detection of landslide processes and subsurface hazard identification.

Heritage Mapping

Non-invasive 3D mapping of ancient necropolises, architectural foundations and cultural monuments for public and private institutions.

OPERATING MODEL

How an Aerial Survey Works

Autonomous Flight

Platforms fly fully autonomous patterns using automated terrain following to hold a constant, safe altitude above the ground.

Multi-Sensor Capture

Ground-penetrating radar and magnetometric data are collected simultaneously for complete spectrum coverage.

3D Processing

Raw data is analyzed in specialized software (UgCS, GeoHammer, MAGNETO) to generate three-dimensional subsurface maps in real time.

Secure Delivery

Results are handled in full compliance with state and sovereign security standards for critical and sensitive information.

UNMANNED SYSTEMS

Heavy-Lift Multi-Rotor Platforms

Our unmanned systems are built around heavy-lift multi-rotor platforms able to carry specialized payloads for aerial geophysical operations. Fully autonomous flight with terrain following keeps sensors at the optimal height above uneven ground.

  • Heavy-lift multi-rotor aircraft
  • Specialized GPR and magnetometer payloads
  • Aerial geophysical operations in difficult terrain
  • Detailed platform specifications available to qualified clients on request
